## Book p633 — 04.pdf PDF8 — Ch45

- **Clinical presentation :** flow — sensory symptoms (B/L tingling & numbness of lower limbs : rubbery legs) → mild transient bowel & bladder dysfunction → motor symptoms (bilateral lower limb weakness).
- **Examination findings :** HMF : normal; cranial nerves : normal; motor (UL) : normal; bilateral atonic areflexic paraparesis (proximal + distal) of lower limbs; large fibre (Aα) sensory loss.
- **Progression of disease :** rapidly progressive — ≥24 hours since incident → areflexic ascending paralysis (lower limb → upper limb) → cranial nerve involvement (30% affected, 4–15% mortality) → brainstem involvement → respiratory centre affected → requires ventilation.
- **Prognosis :** 80% recover (of which 5–10% have persistent motor weakness); 30% → lower cranial nerve & brainstem involvement → dysphagia/dysarthria → aspiration → death; survival rate : 85–90% (early intervention).
- **Bad prognostic factors (ANS manifestations) :** 1. labile HTN (extreme BP fluctuations) → PRES (posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome); 2. postural hypotension; 3. arrhythmias; 4. deep aching pain in affected muscles.
- **Note :** D/d for neuropathy with PRES : porphyrias.

- **Inciting factors :** 1. Gastroenteritis/URTI (past 4 weeks) : in 60–70% GBS cases. 2. Associated infections : C. jejuni, CMV, EBV, mycoplasma, Hep A/B, HIV. 3. Vaccination associated. 4. Molecular mimicry.

## Book p642 — 04.pdf PDF17 — Ch47 title page "MUSCULAR DYSTROPHIES"

- **Dystrophin Defect :** structural protein involvement; necrosis of muscle → replaced by adipose & fibrous tissue; X linked deletion; Rx : steroids. Photograph captioned "Lordosis" with marker arrows A (trunk) and B (knee/leg).
- **Duchenne's Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) :** progressive muscle degeneration; age timeline 0–18 years:
  - ~0–1 : gross motor delay (walking late).
  - ~2–4 : frequent falls → presents to clinic.
  - ~5–7 : toe walking; Gower's sign; cramps unrelated to exercise; lordosis.
  - ~8–10 : supported walking.
  - ~11–12 : wheelchair bound.
  - ~13–14 : upper limb involvement.
  - ~16–18 : respiratory involvement → aspiration pneumonia : m/c cause of death.
- **Additional involvement :** scoliosis → interstitial lung disease (ILD); pseudohypertrophy of muscle (calf > other muscles); mental retardation; cardiomyopathies; nocturnal hypoventilation → pooling of secretions → aspiration.
- **Becker's Muscular Dystrophy :** cause of death : cardiac (dilated cardiomyopathy); creatine kinase, electromyography, biopsy : similar to DMD; presentation : 5–60 years; survival : >40 years; "milder than DMD" : contracture absent; less weakness; mental retardation absent.
